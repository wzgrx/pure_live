NotoColorEmoji-Subset.ttf is a subset of Noto Color Emoji 2.057
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, the COLRv1 build served by
Google Fonts) holding only the 40 emoji of the local interaction's gifts and
platform badges (docs/ui/compare/U.2k/src/emoji.woff2 converted to TrueType).
