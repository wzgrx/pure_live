NotoColorEmoji-Subset.ttf is a subset of Noto Color Emoji 2.057
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, the COLRv1 build served by
Google Fonts) holding only the 74 emoji of the local interaction's gifts and
platform badges: the 40 of docs/ui/compare/U.2k/src/emoji.woff2 and the 34
the platform gifts of D08.6 added (fetched from the Google Fonts API with
text=, the SVG table dropped, subset with fontTools).
